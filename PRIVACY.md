# Presence Studio privacy notice

**English** · [한국어](PRIVACY.ko.md)

Updated: November 1, 2026. Applies to the Presence Studio browser extension 1.4.0 and Windows app 2.9.0.

Presence Studio is maintained by [LUNA12379](https://github.com/LUNA12379). It lets you share the media you choose to play on YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud with your Discord activity and optional desktop/OBS overlays. The extension requires the separate Windows app. There is no Presence Studio account, advertising service, analytics SDK or developer-operated collection server.

## What the extension processes

On its declared YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud sites, the extension reads the current media title, creator/artist, public video or track identifier/URL, cover-image URL, playback position/duration, playback rate, pause/ad/live/end state, and whether that media tab is active. It does not read unrelated websites, account passwords, cookies, payment information, private messages or browser-wide history. Incognito access is disabled. Private SoundCloud track links are not supported.

This supported-site playback information is website content and browsing activity. It is sent to the app on the same computer at `http://127.0.0.1:49832`; the extension does not send it to a developer server. The app keeps live playback sessions in memory, not in a watch-history database.

The extension stores a random browser-profile ID, a pairing credential and your popup-language preference in extension-local storage. They are not synced to your browser account. The profile ID keeps tabs in different browser profiles separate and is not sent to Discord. The one-time pairing code and resulting credential authenticate the extension to the local app; they are not your Google, SoundCloud or Discord login details. The desktop app saves a hash of the store-extension credential with its browser identity and origin. Content scripts cannot access that credential storage.

## Sharing with Discord and overlays

After you pair the extension, playback information is sent to the local app while it runs, even if Discord activity sharing is off. When **Share activity** is enabled and an automatic media activity is selected, the app sends the selected activity to the Discord desktop client through local IPC. Discord then processes and displays that activity under its own settings and [privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Activity text, media links, cover images and timing may be visible to other people through Discord. Custom activities are controlled separately in the app.

You can turn off **Share activity**, exclude titles/creators using keywords, select a custom activity, or disconnect/disable the extension. The sharing switch and exclusions control what is published; disabling or disconnecting the extension stops its playback reports. Desktop and OBS overlays are optional. Keep the private OBS URL and local image URLs to yourself; an OBS source can be visible in a recording or stream you choose to broadcast.

## Other local storage and network requests

The Windows app stores Application IDs, preferences, presets, imported images and connection information in `%APPDATA%\PresenceStudio`. App updates and ordinary uninstall preserve that folder. These files are not encrypted. The private unpacked extension generated by the app contains a local connection key and must not be shared.

The app checks the project's GitHub release channel when you request an update or enable startup checks. It also requests a small public store-availability file when its interface starts, so installation reminders are shown only after the extension is released. These requests contain no media titles, playback history, pairing keys or Application IDs. GitHub receives ordinary connection metadata such as an IP address and applies its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Installers can obtain WebView2 from Microsoft when missing. Loading thumbnails, Discord assets or an external image you configured can contact that image host. Those providers apply their own policies.

## Retention and control

- Live playback sessions expire when they stop reporting, or are removed on disconnect or app exit. No playback-history database is maintained.
- In **Connections & settings → Browser extension**, disconnect a paired browser to revoke its credential and clear its live sessions. The extension popup also has **Disconnect**.
- Uninstalling the extension removes its extension-local data. If you uninstall it without disconnecting first, remove its saved pairing from the app separately.
- Presets, imported images and settings remain locally until you remove them. For a complete reset, close the app and remove `%APPDATA%\PresenceStudio` after saving any personal backups you want to keep.
- Information already displayed through Discord or included in your own recordings is subject to those services and your own sharing choices.

Presence Studio does not sell user data or use it for ads, credit decisions or purposes unrelated to the activity-sharing features described here. No human review of your playback information by the project is built into the app. If you voluntarily include logs or screenshots in a support issue, review them first and remove private information.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open an issue at [presence-studio](https://github.com/LUNA12379/presence-studio/issues). For credentials or security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/LUNA12379/presence-studio/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Do not post tokens, personal configuration files or private activity screenshots in public issues. Updates to this notice will be committed with a revised date.
